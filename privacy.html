Privacy Policy

App: Greeting  ·  Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Greeting application (the "App") collects, why, and how it is handled. The App is a personal publishing tool used to upload video files to a TikTok account that the user has authorised.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhatWhy
TikTok authorisation dataOAuth access token and refresh token, granted scopes, and the TikTok open ID / display name returned by the APITo upload videos to the authorised account and to show you which account is connected
Content you uploadVideo files and their metadata (title, caption, privacy setting) that you choose to publishTo deliver the upload to TikTok on your instruction
Technical logsTimestamps, API response codes, and errorsTo debug failed uploads and keep the App working

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your TikTok password, your contacts, your device location, or your browsing history. The App does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

3. How information is used

Data is used solely to perform the upload you requested and to report the result back to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

4. Where data is stored

Authorisation tokens and upload logs are stored locally on the machine where the App runs. If a hosted component is used, tokens are stored in the corresponding account's private storage and are never made public.

5. Data sharing

The only external service the App sends data to is TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com), strictly for the upload operation you initiate. TikTok's own handling of that data is described in TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Authorisation tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or revoke access.
  • Upload logs are kept for a maximum of 90 days and then deleted.
  • To request deletion of all data held by the App, email us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.
  • Revoking the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) immediately stops all further access.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Security

Tokens are kept private and transmitted only over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the data we hold.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
